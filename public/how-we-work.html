Trusted advisors to the leaders who own the results.

A typical engagement

Start small. See results. Build from there.

  1. Launch
    We learn your goals and what you have tried before, then set up the measure and a plan for telling employees.
  2. Measure
    Employees complete the WAIOS™ Wellbeing Measure privately and see their own results.
  3. Map
    Your executive team sees the heat maps and the drivers behind each score.
  4. Debrief and act
    We agree priorities with your leaders, then workshops and manager training focus on the drivers that matter most.
  5. Re-measure
    We measure again to show what changed, and keep advising as your organization changes.
Each cycle ends with a re-measure, so progress is always tracked against your baseline.

02 · The framework

One measure. Five scores.

The WAIOS™ Framework follows the path work takes into a person's life: from how engaged they are in the work itself, to whether it is burning them out, to the environment around them, to how work shapes their life, to how their life is going overall. Each tier has its own score.

04 · How it works

From baseline score to a clear plan.

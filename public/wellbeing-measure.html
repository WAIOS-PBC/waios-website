01 · The framework

Five tiers. One Wellbeing Score.

The WAIOS™ Framework follows the path work takes into a person's life: from how engaged they are in the work itself, to whether it is burning them out, to the environment around them, to how work shapes their life, to how their life is going overall. Each tier has its own score, and together they produce one Wellbeing Score.

Is this person emotionally connected to their work? Measured first, because if someone is in the wrong role, every other change is harder.

02 · What we ask

The questions your people have been waiting for someone to ask.

A sample from the measure. These are the conditions employees talk about with each other but rarely get asked about by their employer.

Work EngagementRole alignment
“The nature of my work aligns with my skills and interests.”
People in the wrong role struggle no matter how good the environment is.
BurnoutWorkload
“My workload feels manageable.”
An unmanageable workload is one of the core drivers of burnout.
BurnoutFairness
“Decisions that affect me are made transparently.”
People can accept hard decisions. Hidden ones erode trust.
Work EnvironmentWork-life harmony
“To what extent do you feel obligated to remain available and responsive to work-related communication (e.g., email, messaging platforms, calls) outside of your scheduled working hours?”
The always-on expectation rarely shows up in an engagement survey.
Work EnvironmentFair pay
“I feel that my compensation is fair for the work I do.”
Pay that feels unfair wears people down quietly.
Work EnvironmentPsychological safety
“I feel safe speaking up at work.”
Teams that cannot raise problems cannot fix them.
Work EnvironmentRelationship with manager
“My manager encourages me to prioritize my wellbeing.”
Managers give, or withhold, permission to recover.
Work WellbeingThe organization cares
“I believe my company genuinely cares about my wellbeing.”
Whether people feel cared for shapes everything else.
Overall WellbeingEmotional health
“Over the past two weeks, I have felt overwhelmed by stress or low mood.”
Life outside work shows up at work, in both directions.
Validated items are asked word for word. Answers are private, and no individual response is ever shared with an employer.

04 · How it works

From baseline score to a clear plan.

  1. Step 1
    Launch
    We set up the measure, your context questions and a communication plan for employees.

    We measure again to show what changed and where to focus next.

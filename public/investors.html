What we are building

The Wellbeing AI Operating System™

WAIOS™ will measure wellbeing continuously, explain what is driving it, route the right action to the person who can change it, and show the effect in the numbers a business already runs on. The WAIOS™ Wellbeing Measure is where it begins.

It measures what matters most
A live Wellbeing Score for every person, team and organization, in place of the annual survey.
It acts on what it measures
Evidence-based action for employees, managers and leaders, at the level where each can change something.
It gives every person something back
A private picture of their own wellbeing that only they can see.

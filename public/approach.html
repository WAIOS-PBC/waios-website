Our approach

Support from the top down and the ground up, at the same time.

Leaders get the data and the strategy to fix what causes burnout. Every employee gets a practical guide to protect their own wellbeing at work. Nobody has to wait for the system to change before they get help.

Top down

For leaders

See where burnout is building, understand why, and act on the causes rather than the symptoms.

  • Heat maps by team and department
    Wellbeing, engagement and burnout risk in one view, tracked over time.
  • The drivers behind every score
    Workload, recovery, connection and the other conditions that move the numbers.
  • Expert-led debriefs and strategy
    Our founders and experts help leaders build a prevention plan and hold it in place.
  • Progress tied to performance
    Show the effect on turnover, absence and goals, so the investment can be defended.
Ground up

For every employee

Everyone who takes the measure gets something back, and the tools to use it at work.

  • Private personal results
    Their own picture of wellbeing and burnout risk, seen only by them.
  • A strategic guide
    A clear plan for their own wellbeing, grounded in the research on what prevents burnout.
  • Practical applications at work
    Small, specific changes they can make in their workday, their team and their conversations.
  • Access to experts
    Check-ins, accountability and expert support when they need it, in person or virtually.

Why both

Burnout is a systemic problem. Fixing the root causes takes time. People need support so that change can happen.

Top down alone is too slow
Strategy and structural change take months. Employees struggling today cannot wait for them.
Bottom up alone puts it on the individual
Self-care cannot fix a broken workload. Without leadership action, the causes stay in place.
Together, it holds
Leaders change the conditions, employees build the habits, and the next measure shows both.

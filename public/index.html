The WAIOS™ Framework

One measure. Five scores.

The WAIOS™ Framework follows the path work takes into a person’s life, from how engaged they are in the work itself to how their life is going overall. Each tier has its own score.

  1. Tier 1
    Work Engagement
    Is this person emotionally connected to their work?
  2. Tier 2
    Burnout
    Is the work sustainable?
  3. Tier 3
    Work Environment
    Is the environment helping or holding this person back?
  4. Tier 4
    Work Wellbeing
    Is work making this person’s life better or worse?
  5. Tier 5
    Overall Wellbeing
    Setting work aside, how is this person’s life going?

How it works

From a baseline score to a clear plan.

  1. Step 1
    Launch
    We set up the measure, your context questions and a communication plan for employees.
  2. Step 2
    Measure
    Employees complete it privately in less than 10 minutes. Every person sees their own results.
  3. Step 3
    Map
    You receive heat maps by team and department, with the drivers behind each score.
  4. Step 4
    Debrief and act
    Our founders walk your leaders through the results and build a focused action plan.
  5. Step 5
    Re-measure
    We measure again to show what changed and where to focus next.
Two tracks, one program. While leaders work on the system, employees work on what is in their control.

01 · How we see burnout

The whole person, in context.

Burnout rarely has one cause. We look at what’s happening to the person, in their life, at work, and in the world around them, so every fix targets what’s actually driving chronic stress.

1The person
Energy, focus, resilience under load, and whether they still feel connected to what they do.
2Their life
Caregiving, health, finances, the commute. What they carry in with them every morning.
3Their work
Workload, recovery, control, fairness, recognition and psychological safety. The everyday conditions that drive burnout.
4The world around them
Their industry, culture and community, and what’s happening in society and the economy.
